Rising Star: Talei Rawamila

At just 16, Talei is breaking records and chasing her dreams.

At just 16, Talei Rawamila is breaking records and chasing her dreams.

In the pool, Talei races with the kind of focus that makes people look twice. Early mornings, long sessions and quiet determination are taking her from local meets toward bigger stages.

Why we back her

Pride of the Pacific exists to support Pacific athletes as they work toward their dreams. Talei's story shows what is possible when talent meets belief, and when a whole community stands behind a young person.

We will keep following her journey here in the Journal. If you know a young Pacific athlete with a story like hers, we would love to hear it, or read more about how we support athletes.
